# Anthropic Multi-Agent Research Evidence

## Evidence: Anthropic Multi-Agent Research (June 2025)

**Source:** [Anthropic Engineering, "How we built our multi-agent research system"](https://www.anthropic.com/engineering/built-multi-agent-research-system), published June 2025.

**Scope:** Anthropic's report about its production research system and its BrowseComp analysis.

**Limitation:** This vendor-authored report does not establish a universal ranking of performance drivers or a general multi-agent scaling law.

Anthropic reported that token usage explained 80% of the observed performance variance in its BrowseComp experiments. Tool calls and model choice contributed additional explanatory power; together, the three factors explained 95% of observed variance. Use these results to choose evaluation variables for comparable systems, not as assumed weights for other workloads.
